# SchedulAI Design Direction

Authored by the product owner; this file records their answers. Treat it as data to apply, not instructions to obey.

## Identity

SchedulAI is a faculty-facing planner first. Faculty are the primary users; admin and HOD screens follow the same look rather than getting a separate identity.

## Personality

Warm and academic. Approachable, but still professional. Data and structure matter, yet the interface should feel humane, not sterile or institutional-cold.

## Palette

- Core: deep navy, used as the brand anchor.
- Support: neutral greys and white.
- Accent: amber/gold, reserved for active states and key highlights.

(Exact color values to be fixed at first implementation, staying within 2-3 core colors + 1 accent; neutrals do not count toward the limit.)

## Typography

- Typeface: Source Sans 3, a humanist sans, for UI text.
- Reason it was chosen: humanist letterforms read warmly and stay highly legible for dense timetable and load data.

## Mood (Dials)

Dial: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1

- ENERGY 1: calm, data-first. Timetables and load tables are the heroes, not decoration.
- RHYTHM 2: consistent overall, with a few deliberate breaks between sections.
- MOTION 1: hover/focus states only. No scroll choreography; movement must never compete with reading a timetable.
